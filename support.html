Support

An icon like the one from Sary-Bulak costs about 180,000 som to treat: materials, solvents, gold leaf and eight months of a conservator’s time. Every gift goes to the next object in the queue.

Amount

Gifts are received by the Körköm Foundation, reg. No. 0412-NKO, for the Conservation Workshop. Gifts of 180,000 som or more are named in the treatment report, if you wish.
