An icon from Sary-Bulak, under a hundred years of varnish

The parish brought it to us in March, almost black. Eight months of swabs later, the robe is red again.

Object
KW-2026-014, icon of the Virgin on a pine panel, late 19th century
Size
41 × 31 × 2.4 cm
Owner
Parish church, Sary-Bulak
Treatment
Varnish removed with solvent gels; flaking gold leaf laid down; losses filled and retouched so they can be told apart up close
Conservator
Aida Sultanbekova

The drawing shows what the cleaning does. Photographs of every stage are in the treatment report, which the parish keeps.
