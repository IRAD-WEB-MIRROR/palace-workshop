Finding the workshop

The workshop is off the courtyard of the Irad Palace of Art, Cathedral Street 4, Iyık Janyl. Use the courtyard gate on the left of the main entrance. Free assessments on Thursdays, 10:00–13:00; bring the object wrapped in a clean sheet. On the map.

School and student visits on the first Wednesday of the month, through the Palace of Art.
